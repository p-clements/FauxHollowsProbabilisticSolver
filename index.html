How to Use

  1. Mark the diagonal half of the board the game shows as Blocked.
  2. Select Game 1 or Game 2 in the Advanced menu to match where you are in the minigame.
  3. As you flip cards, select the matching picker item (Missed, Coffer, Swords, Fox) and click the cell.
  4. The solver recalculates after every mark and highlights the best cell to flip next with a gold crosshair.

How It Scores Cells

Faux Hollows always places its four pieces (fox, coffer, swords, blocked cells) in fixed shapes on the 6x6 board. Every time you mark a cell, the solver narrows down every board layout still consistent with what you've seen, and turns that into a probability that each remaining cell hides the fox, coffer, or swords. Those probabilities are combined using the weights in the Advanced menu, and cells are also zeroed out once you no longer have enough flips left to complete the shape they'd belong to.
