    How to Use

    1. Mark the diagonal half of the board the game shows as Blocked (does not use flips).
    2. Select First Retelling or Second Retelling above the picker to match where you are in the minigame.
    3. As you flip cards, select the matching picker item (Missed, Coffer, Swords, Fox) and click the cell.
    4. The solver recalculates after every mark and highlights the best cell to flip next with a ring. Read the status panel under the board for why.

    Tips: Long-press a cell to erase it without switching to Erase. Use Undo for the last mark. On phones, the tool picker stays near the board.

    How It Chooses

    Once all 5 blocked tiles are marked, the solver matches them against the community spreadsheet of recorded boards. That leaves a handful of possible layouts, and every flip you record rules more of them out. The solver then plans the rest of the game ahead: it picks the flip that earns the most on average across those layouts, using the values in the Advanced menu, within your 11 flips.

    If a board isn't in the spreadsheet, it falls back to fitting the shapes onto the board and picking the best single flip. A piece only pays out once every one of its cells is flipped, so a piece that needs more cells than you have flips left is outlined and ignored.

    Cells the solver is certain about are filled in with a dashed border. They don't use your flips until you flip them for real: select the matching picker item and click the cell. Leave them for last, because they can't tell you anything new.
